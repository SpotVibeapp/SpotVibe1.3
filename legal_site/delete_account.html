How to delete your account and data

Option 1 — In the app (fastest):

Open SpotVibe, go to Profile → Delete Account, confirm, and enter your password if you signed up with email. This permanently deletes your account, profile, events, RSVPs, and comments.

Option 2 — By email:

Send an email to blakejohnson@spotvibeapp.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Account deletion request.” We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days, except where we must keep records (for example a paid invoice or a legal hold).

What gets deleted

Your account, profile (name, email, avatar), saved events, RSVPs, comments, the events you created, and your venue-claim submissions are removed. Event content you posted may remain in backups for a limited time before being purged.

Subscriptions

Deleting your SpotVibe account does not cancel a SpotVibe Premium subscription. To stop being billed, cancel the subscription in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings first, then delete your SpotVibe account.
