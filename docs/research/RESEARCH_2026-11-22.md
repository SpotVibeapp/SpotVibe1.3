## 0. What changed since Nov 9 (headline)

   - Bending Spoons closed its Eventbrite purchase on Mar 10, 2026 and cut "a large portion" of staff ([8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1475115/000114036126008636/ef20067526_8k.htm); [Eventbrite blog](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/whats-next-at-eventbrite/)) [Fact].

   - **85% of Meetup's latest 500 US App Store written reviews are 1–2★, and 53% of those negative reviews mention billing** [Data, §4].
2. **Hosts and listings now monetize through payments and add-ons, not paywalls on posting.**
   - Partiful launched ticketing in June 2026 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/partiful-launches-ticketing-bringing-paid-events-into-the-social-platform-where-people-already-make-plans-302789410.html)) and an optional Partiful Plus subscription in Oct 2026 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/partiful-launches-partiful-plus-subscription-with-new-customization-features-302907923.html)). Creating events and RSVPs stay free [Fact].
   - Luma grew about 3x in 2026 to eight-figure ARR with 7 people ([Inc. via Maven](https://inc-1-smart-business-story.beehiiv.com/p/meet-the-7-person-startup-powering-silicon-valley-s-event-boom)) [Est., company-sourced].
3. **Big platforms moved into local discovery.** This is a new threat since Nov 9.
   - TikTok launched a US **Local Feed** on Feb 11, 2026 ([TikTok](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-local-feed?lang=en); [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/877161/tiktok-us-launches-local-feeds)) [Fact].

   - Pie changed its feed to show *who you know* going. Time in app rose from 2 to 10 minutes. It then pivoted to "Community Homes" ([TechCrunch, Aug 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/andy-dunns-startup-pie-becomes-less-of-an-events-app-and-more-of-a-social-network/)) [Fact, company-reported].

   - Apple updated guideline 1.2 on Oct 16, 2026 (UGC moderation now explicitly covers AI and nonconsensual imagery) ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=vvzx6cul)) [Fact].
   - **Texas SB 2420 (App Store Accountability Act) is enforceable while the injunction is stayed (since Jun 4, 2026).** SpotVibe's launch metro is in Texas ([tracker](https://texasdatalaw.com/tracker/sb-2420/); [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=sg176nne)) [Fact].

## 2. Market size and trends

All market sizes are vendor estimates with different definitions. Treat them as direction, not truth.
- "Event discovery platforms" (platform revenue, global): **$6.8B in 2025 → $15.4B by 2034, 9.5% CAGR**.
  - 70% comes from ticket and transaction fees.
  - Advertising and promoted listings are the fastest-growing slice (12.2%/yr).
  - Source: [Dataintelo](https://dataintelo.com/report/event-discovery-platforms-market) [Est.].
- "Local event discovery platforms": $7.6B in 2025 → $17.0B by 2032 ([Stratistics via GII](https://www.gii.tw/report/smrc1876744-local-event-discovery-platforms-market-forecasts.html)) [Est.].
- "Hyperlocal event discovery": $2.8B in 2026 ([Stratistics](https://www.strategymrc.com/report/hyperlocal-event-discovery-platforms-market)) [Est.].
- Eventbrite, the largest pure-play, shrank before its sale.
  - Q3 2025 revenue was $71.7M, down 8% YoY. Paid tickets were down 3%.
  - **Eventbrite Ads grew 38% YoY** ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1475115/000147511525000114/ebq32025earningspressrelea.htm)) [Fact].
  - Small hosts grew faster than large ones (Q3 2025 call, [transcript](https://exa.ai/library/markets/stocks/EB/earnings/FY2025/Q3/transcript)) [Fact].
- **Demand-side trend.**
  - Eventbrite's 2026 Social Study: 69% of 18–35s rely on friends and word of mouth, and 53% prefer suggestions from people they follow ([Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/press/newsroom/eventbrites-inaugural-social-study-report-reveals-the-reset-to-real/)) [Fact, vendor survey].
  - Gen Z discovers events through Instagram (48%) and TikTok (30%) ([TRNDS 2025](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Eventbrite-TRNDS-2025-US.pdf)) [Fact, vendor survey].
- **Read [Opinion].** The money is in transactions and promotion, and the growth is in small hosts and trusted social proof. Both favor a host-first local app, provided it reaches density.

**Cross-check.**
- An independent pull agrees on Partiful: 10% 1–2★ in its last 500 ([Makerio](https://reviewlist.makerio.net/invitation-wishlist-apps-reviews/)).
- Meetup: 31% of the last 809 reviews mention billing ([AppsLupa](https://appslupa.com/app/meetup-social-events-and-groups/id375990038)).

**What the complaints say [Opinion, from the data above].**
1. **Money friction dominates the worst-rated apps** (Meetup, Fever, DICE, Timeleft, Bumble BFF). The causes are paywalls, unclear charges, hard cancellation and refunds.
   - SpotVibe charges hosts, not attendees, and never takes ticket money. Keep it that way, and keep the paywall copy plain.
2. **Free, host-first tools are the best loved** (Partiful 11%, Luma 16%, Heylo 19%).
3. **Listing quality**: irrelevant, spam, fake, or wrong city or date (Eventbrite discovery, AllEvents, Meetup, Nextdoor).
   - The answer is organizer-confirmed listings, working reports, and freshness.
4. **Notifications and email spam** come up across the board. Fewer, relevant alerts win.
5. **Ghosting and dead groups**: Bumble BFF groups ([r/bumblebff](https://www.reddit.com/r/bumblebff/comments/1x88hxk/any_experience_managing_a_group_on_bff/)).
   - Recurring, hosted events beat open-ended groups.

## 5. Design and UX practice

- **Show social proof and the people, not just the listing.**
  - Pie's switch to showing who's attending raised time in app from 2 to 10 minutes ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/andy-dunns-startup-pie-becomes-less-of-an-events-app-and-more-of-a-social-network/)) [Fact, company-reported].
  - Partiful gates the guest list behind an RSVP to build curiosity and commitment ([teardown](https://daltonhertel.com/partiful-teardown.html)) [Opinion, practitioner].
- **No-app-required pages for guests.**
  - Partiful's link works without an install ([NoGood](https://nogood.io/blog/partiful-marketing-strategy/)).
  - Partiful's own 1–2★ reviews complain about being "forced to download" [Data: "download" appears in 7 of 52].
  - SpotVibe's `/app/event/<id>` web page and `/app/submit` already fit this.
- **Empty states must offer an action.** Never show a blank list: widen the radius, say the area is new, invite people to post first ([Pulselink](https://pulselink.in/blog/befree-cold-start-problem); [Plancast post-mortem](https://techcrunch.com/2012/01/22/post-mortem-for-plancast/)). SpotVibe already does this (MARKET_RISKS §1).
- **Trust signals.**
  - Eventbrite added Top Organizer badges and richer organizer profiles in Apr 2026 ([blog](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/whats-next-at-eventbrite/)).
  - Luma's host approval was its early edge ([Inc.](https://inc-1-smart-business-story.beehiiv.com/p/meet-the-7-person-startup-powering-silicon-valley-s-event-boom)).
- **Offline to online.** Eventbrite's new printable flyer has a QR code "for venue windows, local boards" ([blog](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/whats-next-at-eventbrite/)). **Built today for SpotVibe (§14).**
- **Rating prompts.** Prompt only after a successful action, never during onboarding or after an error, and don't gate the prompt ([Android In-App Review](https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-review)) [Fact].
- **Blake's UI rules stay** [Fact, Blake preference]:
  - compact top-bar icons for Ask and search
  - a slim 56–60 dp nav
  - a full event card visible on load
  - layouts checked on the Z Fold cover and inner screens.

## 7. Growth channels

1. **Host-shared links and invites.**
   - The dominant loop at Partiful, Luma and Posh ([NoGood](https://nogood.io/blog/partiful-marketing-strategy/)).
   - Luma: "you host an event, and people know about it, so it's growing on its own" ([Inc.](https://inc-1-smart-business-story.beehiiv.com/p/meet-the-7-person-startup-powering-silicon-valley-s-event-boom)) [Fact].
2. **Offline QR at the venue.** Eventbrite's flyer tool targets walk-ups [Fact]. Cheap for coordinators, and it turns every poster into an install path.
3. **Short-form local video.**
   - TikTok Local Feed (US, Feb 2026) ranks by location, topic and recency ([TikTok](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-local-feed?lang=en)) [Fact].
   - Instagram Map surfaces location-tagged Reels ([Meta](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-new-features-connect-friends)) [Fact].
   - This supports the fixed plan of **real game-night clips, location-tagged to the venue**.
4. **Organic search for event pages.**
   - Google still supports Event rich results for public, physically located events, one event per page ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/event)) [Fact].
   - Eventbrite lists "better SEO for event listings" as a 2026 pillar [Fact].
   - The SpotVibe web app is a JavaScript single-page app, so crawlers that don't run JavaScript see an empty page. **Built today: server-rendered `/e/<id>` pages (§14), not deployed.**
5. **Community seeding and venue partnerships.**
   - Run clubs and board-game nights grow through recurring hosts and WhatsApp/Discord ([Strava](https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-releases-12th-annual-year-in-sport-trend-report-2025); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/game-on-india-board-game-culture-market-growth-2025-125102700144_1.html)) [Fact].
   - Partiful paid campus ambassadors party stipends (Nov 9 doc).
   - Outreach stays on hold per Blake.
6. **Local newsletters and city guides** (Axios Local, 6AM City) earn from sponsorships; events are secondary revenue ([Regional Newsletter](https://regionalnewsletter.com/local-newsletter-business-model)) [Est.]. A future partner channel ("this week on SpotVibe" feed for a newsletter) [Opinion].
7. **Paid app store ads** are now costlier to ignore. Apple added more search ad slots in March 2026 ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/apple-ads-adds-more-ad-slots-to-app-store-search-results-467905)) [Fact]. Not recommended before density [Opinion].

## 8. Store ranking and ASO

- **Apple ranks on text relevance** (name, subtitle, keywords, primary category) **plus behavior** (downloads, ratings and reviews) ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)) [Fact]. Screenshot text indexing is unproven ([ConsultMyApp test](https://www.consultmyapp.com/blog/-is-apple-now-indexing-screenshot-titles-on-the-app-store)) [Est.]. Put "local events", "things to do", "game night" and "run club" in the title, subtitle and keywords.
- **Google Play ranks on relevance, quality, popularity and editorial signals** ([Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9958766?hl=en)).
  - **Core vitals thresholds: crash rate < 1.09%, ANR rate < 0.47%.** Above them, the app can lose visibility and get listing warnings ([Android vitals](https://developer.android.com/google/play/vitals)) [Fact].
  - Engagement (retention, uninstall rate) also feeds quality ([Android](https://developer.android.com/quality/core-value/user-metrics)) [Fact].
- **Ratings:** SpotVibe still has **0 ratings** on Play (Nov 16 plan). Recent ratings weigh more in the Play score ([Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/138230?hl=en)) [Fact]. PR #9 (rating prompt) is still unmerged.

## 10. Adjacent-field lessons (what transfers)

- **Dating and friendship apps.**
  - Paywalls on connection get punished in reviews (Bumble BFF 83%, Timeleft 52% 1–2★).
  - Group and IRL formats are where they're heading (Tinder Group Hangouts).
  - → Keep attendee features free. Charge hosts for reach and tools only.
- **Hobby communities (run clubs, board games).**
  - Growth comes from recurring hosts and repeat weekly attendance (Strava clubs 4x).
  - → Weekly series must be first-class: one post, auto-next-date (PR #14), and a reminder before each date.
- **Ticketing.**
  - Refunds and support are the top pain once money flows (Fever, DICE, Posh).
  - → Don't add payments until support capacity exists.
- **Local news and city guides.**
  - Nextdoor pushes nearby events into feeds (+46% views) and is building recurring events.
  - → Recurring local events are a known gap that SpotVibe can own first in its metro.
- **Creator and organizer tools.**
  - Eventbrite's April 2026 roadmap (profile, badge, flyer, update alerts, SEO) is a ready checklist of what hosts value.
