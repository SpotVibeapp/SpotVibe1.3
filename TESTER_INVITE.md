## LONG VERSION (email / group message)

**Subject: Help me test my new app, SpotVibe 🎉**

Hi! Thanks so much for agreeing to test my app, **SpotVibe**.

**What is SpotVibe?**
SpotVibe helps you discover local events and hidden-gem spots happening near you —
concerts, festivals, markets, community events, nightlife, and the smaller local
venues that usually get overlooked. Think of it as a friendly local "what's going
on around me" app.

**What I need from you:**
Just install it, use it like a normal app for a few minutes, and tell me what you
think — what you liked, anything confusing, and any bugs or crashes.

**How to install it (about 2 minutes):**

1. **On your Android phone**, make sure you're signed in to the Google account you
   gave me (the one you use for the Play Store).
2. Tap this link: **PASTE_YOUR_OPT_IN_LINK_HERE**
3. Tap **"Become a tester"** (or "Accept").
4. Then tap **"Download it on Google Play"** — this opens the SpotVibe page in the
   Play Store.
5. Tap **Install**, like any normal app.

*Note: if the Play Store says the app isn't available or "item not found," don't
worry — it can take up to a couple of hours after you join for it to show up. Just
wait a bit and try the link again.*

**Once it's installed, try these things:**
- Allow **location** when asked, so it can show events near you.
- Browse the main feed of events.
- Tap an event to see its details.
- Try the **map view** and the **search**.
- **Save/bookmark** an event you like.
- If anything looks broken or confusing, take a screenshot!

**How to send me feedback:**
Just reply to me directly (text/email) with anything you noticed — good or bad. Even
"this was confusing" is super helpful. Screenshots of any bugs are gold.

Thank you so much — this really helps me get SpotVibe ready. 🙏
— Blake

## TIPS FOR YOU (Blake) — not part of the message

- **The opt-in link** is in Play Console → Test and release → Testing → Closed
  testing → your Beta track → **Testers** tab → "Copy link".
- Testers **must** join with the exact Google account email you added to the
  "SpotVibe testers" list, or they'll get "item not found".
- After a tester joins, allow up to a few hours for the Play Store to show them the
  app — this delay is normal right after publishing.

  track's Countries/regions, or testers there can't see it.
- Add **your own** testing Gmail to the list too, so you can install the real store
  build on your SM-F956U for the device checks (SG/JamBase badges, share links,
